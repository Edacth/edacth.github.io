Furniture Pokemon #2.



## Enemies can think I gave foes the ability to pick a move to use against friends. Beforehand, I was picking the move for the foe as well as the friend. I was a little bit trepidatious going into this work but it turned out to be pretty easy. I’m really proud of myself for building such a robust battle system. The battle system actually doesn’t know the difference between a real player’s choice and an artificial player’s choice so I could easily put an artificial player on both sides of the battle and watch them duke it out. This sort of thing is notoriously tricky to do if you are relying on hardcoding a bunch of player logic.

Thanks for reading, friends.
